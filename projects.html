Baked Biscuit Project List

Culture Kitchen

Our latest and most complete game. Culture Kitchen is a cooking simulator with some tycoon-like elements. Manage ingredients, create dishes, serve customers, and pay your taxes! Culture Kitchen is still in active development, and may change drastically in the future. Our latest build is available to play in the Baked Biscuit Discord.

Lasting Leviathan

Lasting Leviathan was our first successful game jam project. It’s a short fishing tycoon game where the player needs to manage their crew and make enough profit to eventually hunt down the Leviathan. In its current state, the game is very buggy and lacks polish. Even so, Lasting Leviathan was a good test of our skills under pressure and time constraints.

The Flesh Pit

A take on the internet worldbuilding project, “The Mystery Flesh Pit”. This game was intended to be a first person roguelite dungeon crawler, where you would solve puzzles, avoid enemies, collect items, and uncover the larger narrative of the game.

In its current state, the game includes these features: Premade rooms that spawn in random order, basic enemy AI, inventory slot system, flashlight battery, health, basic player movement, and a speedrun timer. The Flesh Pit also has the most complete amount of art assets of any project we’ve made so far, including 15 unique rooms, ~10 items, and 40+ reusable props/assets.

Fishy Fishy

This was our first real attempt at making a full video game, planned to have multiple locations, a full game loop, and a complete story arc. Fishy Fishy was meant to be a fishing tycoon where you would shoot fish, catch them, and sell them for profit. This ended up being too ambitious and we ended up adding multiple disjointed and goofy game mechanics before ending development.

The final game includes these features: Basic FPS Gunplay, Fish killing and catching mechanics, random fish sizes/values, 3 fish types, multiple upgradables (guns, backpack, nets, etc), a full interactable shop and shopkeeper, an apartment, and other random features.

Untitled Horror Game

Baked Biscuits original game. We started this project to test if we could really make a game. It was intended to be a short horror game where you play as a school janitor after hours. This was obviously not a well made game, but it showcases our first attempts at making simple game mechanics.
